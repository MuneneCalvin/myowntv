# Changelog

## Unreleased
- Now playing: a switch in Home Settings shows what is playing as a dark glass pill (album art, title, artist and app, with how far along) in the prices' place on Home and at the top right of the screensaver. Press Down from the corner buttons and OK to open a card with previous, play or pause and next. After a pause or restart it shows the last thing played, dimmed, for a day; OK opens that app (YouTube: plays that video again). It reads the apps' media sessions only (never notifications) and needs the TV's wireless debugging for a one-time grant that is taken back when you switch it off.

## 1.2.0
- Search: a round Search button on the Home screen. Type or say a title; it shows which of your TV's streaming apps have it in your country, opens the app on it, and can save it to My list (needs your free TMDB key).
- The City box in Home Settings now credits Open-Meteo.com and GeoNames, as their licence (CC BY 4.0) asks.

## 1.1.0
- New MyOwnTV icon and banner, also on the Settings screen and the dock tile.
- The glass clock's colon glows softly like a heartbeat (Android 13 and newer).
- The screensaver now shows the same weather effects (rain, snow, storm, fog) and holiday decorations as the home screen.
- Updates: MyOwnTV finds new releases on GitHub itself and offers Install or Later in a notification card on the Home screen (Home Settings > Updates checks by hand). Only the APK from the releases installs updates.
- The screensaver shows a quote in handwriting (Kafka, Dostoevsky, Gibran, Darwish, Wilde; a dozen of the 50 are about love) instead of the crypto prices, changing every 2 minutes. Turn it off with Home Settings > Screensaver quotes.

## 1.0.1
- Fix: wallpapers were black on Android 12 and older TVs. They now show there too, with a flat white clock in place of the glass one (which needs Android 13 or newer).

## 1.0.0
First release.
- Scene wallpapers (13, each on or off, 5 minutes to 3 hours per picture) with a liquid-glass clock.
- Dark glass dock with each app's own glyph, colour on focus.
- TMDB picks and Watchmode deep links (your own free keys), Keep Watching row.
- Weather effects (rain, snow, storm, fog) and holiday decorations.
- Home button and standby wake return to MyOwnTV without showing the Google home; the Google home and setup wizard stay on.
- Removed: Home Assistant, Immich, photo screensaver and My Photos, self-updater, other languages, Fire TV / Samsung leftovers.
